Before you ask

The details, answered.

The questions ops teams usually have, answered up front so the booking conversation stays short.

What does the club need to provide?

A corner of the lounge — that's the ask. The cart arrives ready to work, and placement, power and any venue specifics are locked with your ops team well before the weekend. Load in and load out happen outside hospitality hours, so setup never shares the room with a guest.

What happens if attendance beats the estimate?

Nothing. The rate is agreed before the weekend and covers every guest who walks up — there's no per-drink meter and no revised invoice. A packed Sunday costs the club exactly what it agreed on Monday.

Who's behind the bar?

Twin Fins' own crew — the operators who ran the Champions Club activation last year, now three trained baristas per session, in uniform, making every drink to order.

How do branding approvals work?

Every piece that carries a Formula 1 or club mark — livery, menu, foam stencils, keepsakes — goes to you for sign-off before anything is produced, and is created only under the rights holder's licence and direction. The mockups in this deck are concept artwork for exactly that conversation.

Can rooms be added after the first booking?

Yes. Each lounge stands alone with its own cart, livery and crew, so Paddock Club or Club Nosso can be added at any point before the weekend without touching the Champions Club plan.

A note from Twin Fins

In our own words.

Dear Formula 1 Miami team,

Thank you for making the time for this.

Twin Fins Coffee began in 2024 around a simple idea: the feeling you get paddling out at first light belongs in a cup. We're a mobile espresso bar based in Atlanta — freshly ground beans, top-tier equipment, and a crew that genuinely cares what lands in a guest's hand. Weddings, brand launches and markets across Georgia, and this past May, trackside in the Champions Club at the Miami Grand Prix.

That weekend is still the standard we measure ourselves against. The room was demanding in the best way — a steady line, guests who notice detail, and no room for a burnt shot no matter how long the queue got. Our crew came home knowing exactly what the Champions Club expects, and wanting another go at it.

This deck is that request. Returning to the Champions Club is our priority for 2027 — the same cart, a sharper livery built into the club's own palette, three trained baristas behind the bar so service never slows when a session lets out, and a menu refined around what the room actually drank. The Paddock Club and Club Nosso are costed here too, and we'd welcome more than one room. But we'd be glad simply to be back in the first.

One note on how we work: we use eco-friendly products, source locally wherever we can, and give a percentage of every sale to charities protecting marine wildlife. The ocean gave us the whole idea, so it gets a cut. It doesn't change what's in the cup — it's just how we'd like to show up in your hospitality spaces.

Whatever you decide, thank you for considering us. We'd be honored to pour for your guests again.
