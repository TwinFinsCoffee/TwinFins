07 — The impact

What this actually changes.

Three claims, each labelled by how much it rests on. What is measured already happened. What is expected follows from a thing that is built and dated. What is directional is a trend with two data points, and is written as one.

Already true Measured

Enquiries arrive ready to confirm.

In July the only path was a blank email form and a thread to work out a date. The scheduler now shows the availability you set, from every page, so a request lands complete at any hour and waits for your yes. Every booking is still yours to approve — what's gone is the back-and-forth, not the control.

What it rests on
  • 26 bio link taps in August — the step that did not exist in July
  • 1,163 profile visits looking for a way in
  • 6 pages, every one of them with the modal on it
This month Expected

September's ceiling is higher than August's, and the date is fixed.

The Dragon Con takeover is live for 4–7 September at AmericasMart — a crowd that is downtown, in a buying mood, and does not know the cart yet. August already proved the campaign content travels; this is the weekend it points at something.

What it rests on
  • 7 of 9 top posts in August were Dragon Con content
  • 57.3% of reach was already people who don't follow
  • 4 days on the floor, with the page and the drop ready
The next 90 days Directional

Reach compounds, because the library and the audience now exist.

One month is not a trend. But views rose 27% and non-follower views 41% while the account posted more, and one post put the cart in front of 925 people who chose to pass it on. If the cadence holds, the base it starts from each month is bigger than the last.

What it rests on
  • +27% views and +41% stranger reach, Aug vs July
  • 925 shares on a single organic post
  • +67 followers, an audience that is 67% aged 25–44
