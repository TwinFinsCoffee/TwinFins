Event booking agreement

Get your custom quote for event booking today by sending us a email or call the number below! Please carefully read our booking agreement as stated below. All parties are required to follow each agreement in order to book a Twin Fins Coffee cart. 


  • 24 hour cancellation policy. All parties must cancel within at least 24 hours. Failure to do so will result in a cancellation fee.
  • All bookings must be made at least 5 days prior to event date. 
  • Drinks served may contain dairy, gluten, or nuts. please disclose any allergies before consuming any beverages. 
  • All parties must provide an address, date, and time as well as a contact number when booking an event.
  • $100 deposit is required when booking events over 200 people. The deposit will go towards the cost of the service.
  • for any questions or concerns, please email twinfinscoffee@gmail.com
